01 — Tracks

Pick your lane.
Or do both.

Two courses run side by side, each taught by the officers who actually do that work. One membership covers everything.

Research

Aarav Sinha, Sharvin Goyal

Build models. Publish papers.

You learn how models actually work, train your own, run real experiments, and write the results up for publication — the same pipeline your instructors use at ICML and NeurIPS workshops.

  • Neural networks, reinforcement learning, and LLMs from the ground up
  • Design and run your own experiments, not just tutorials
  • Write the paper: methods, results, figures, the whole thing
  • Submit to high school research symposiums and workshop tracks
Build

Robert Pierson

Vibecode. CAD. Ship it.

Use AI as a tool to make real things fast — full apps, designed parts, working products — and learn enough to stay the engineer instead of the passenger when the AI gets it wrong.

  • Vibecoding with AI agents: ship full apps, then learn to review the output
  • AI-assisted CAD: prompt or sketch to a real, printable, manufacturable part
  • Websites, bots, tools, hardware — portfolio pieces you own
  • Deployment, version control, and how to not lose your work

05 — How to join

Four steps.

The interest form is the only online part. Everything else happens in person at a meeting.

  1. Fill out the interest form

    One form for everyone — members and anyone who wants to be an officer. Open the form. Takes about a minute.

  2. Get the permission slip signed

    Katy ISD requires a signed parent permission slip for every non-curriculum club. Download it here, or find it on the OTHS clubs page.

  3. Bring it to a Wednesday meeting

    Room 1661, 2:45–3:15 PM. Hand the signed slip to Mrs. Dennison. Forms have to be turned in fresh each school year.

  4. Pay your dues in cash

    $20 for general membership, or $30 for membership plus the shirt. Pay at the same meeting you turn in your slip and you're in.
