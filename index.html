01
Two Tracks

Pick your lane. Or do both.

The club runs two courses side by side, each taught by the officers who actually do that work. Show up to either, or both — one membership covers everything.

Track 01 — Research

Build models. Publish papers.

Aarav SinhaSharvin Goyal

The AI course. You learn how models actually work, train your own, run real experiments on them, and write the results up for publication — the same pipeline your instructors use at ICML and NeurIPS workshops.

  • Neural networks, reinforcement learning, and LLMs from the ground up
  • Design and run your own experiments, not just tutorials
  • Write the paper: methods, results, figures, the whole thing
  • Submit to high school research symposiums and workshop tracks
Track 02 — Build

Vibecode. CAD. Ship it.

Robert Pierson

The building course. Use AI as a tool to make real things fast — full apps, designed parts, working products — and learn enough to stay the engineer instead of the passenger when the AI gets it wrong.

  • Vibecoding with AI agents: ship full apps, then learn to review the output
  • AI-assisted CAD: prompt or sketch to a real, printable, manufacturable part
  • Websites, bots, tools, hardware — portfolio pieces you own
  • Deployment, version control, and how to not lose your work
02
What We Do

Not a lecture club. A build club.

Every meeting you either learn a tool, ship something with it, or push a project forward. No prior experience required — we start people from zero.

🧠

Train Real Models

Neural nets, reinforcement learning, and neuroevolution — built and trained from scratch, not just imported. You'll understand what's happening inside.

📄

Publish Research

Run a real project, write it up, submit it. Our officers have papers at ICML and NeurIPS workshops and will walk you through the process.

⚡

Vibecoding

Use AI coding agents to ship full apps fast — and learn where they break, how to review their output, and how to stay in control.

📐

AI-Assisted CAD

Generative and AI-assisted CAD design: go from a prompt or a sketch to a real, manufacturable, printable part.

🔧

Build & Ship

Websites, bots, tools, hardware projects. If you can describe it, we'll help you build it and put it in your portfolio.

🏆

Compete

National and international AI competitions as a team, with officers who've done the research circuit coaching your submissions.

05
How to Join

Four steps. Done in a week.

Start with the interest form — that's the only online part. Everything after happens in person at a Tuesday meeting.

Fill out the interest form

One form for everyone — members and anyone who wants to be an officer. Open the interest form. Takes about a minute.

Fill out the OTHS non-curriculum club form

Katy ISD requires a signed parent permission slip for every non-curriculum club you join. Download it here: OTHS parent permission slip (PDF) — also linked on the OTHS Clubs & Organizations page. Get a parent signature.

Bring it to a Tuesday meeting

Room 1661, 2:45 PM. Hand the signed slip to Mrs. Dennison. Forms have to be turned in fresh each school year.

Pay your dues in cash

$20 for general membership, or $30 for membership plus the official club shirt. Pay at the same meeting you turn in your slip and you're officially in.

Compete & Publish

Where we're taking this

Competitions are where the club shirt becomes mandatory. Research is where a club project turns into something with your name on it, permanently.

Competitions
International Olympiad in AI (IOAI) The Olympics of AI for high schoolers — machine learning and scientific rounds, national team selection. WAICY — World AI Competition for Youth The largest AI competition for ages 13–18, with an AI Showcase track for real-world projects. A good first competition. APEX America High School AI Championship Open to all US high schoolers. No prerequisites, all experience levels — built for first-year members.
Research Venues
IEEE BigData High School Symposium Peer-reviewed original research papers by high school students at a real IEEE conference, with Outstanding Paper and Rising Star awards. AIAS+ Chen Institute Student Showcase Grades 9–12 research posters on AI transforming scientific discovery. Selected students present alongside working researchers.
